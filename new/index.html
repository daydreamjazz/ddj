Daydream Jazz is an independent music festival and cultural platform based in Riga.

Our focus is contemporary jazz, ambient and experimental music, with live performance at the centre of everything we do. The idea is to introduce audiences in Riga to new music while creating intimate and carefully considered experiences around it.
